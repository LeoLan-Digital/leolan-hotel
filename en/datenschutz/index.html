2. General information about data processing

Personal data is processed in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable German data protection law. Personal data is processed only to the extent necessary to provide a functional website, deliver our services, respond to inquiries, and analyze the use of our website where legal consent or another legal basis exists.

3. Server log files and hosting

This website may automatically collect technical information upon access; this includes your IP address, browser type, device information, access date and time, and requested pages. This process is necessary to ensure the stability, security, and presentation of the website.

4. Contact inquiries

If you contact us via email or contact form, we process the information you provide (such as name, email address, phone number, and message content) solely to manage your inquiry and any follow-up communication. The legal basis is GDPR Article 6 (1) lit. b if your request relates to a contract or pre-contractual measures; otherwise, it is based on our legitimate interest in effective communication under GDPR Article 6 (1) lit. f.

5. Cookies and consent management

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies as necessary for technical operation, user preferences, and analytics/marketing integrations. Non-essential cookies are placed only with your consent under GDPR Article 6 (1) lit. a and TTDSG § 25. You can withdraw your consent at any time with future effect via your browser settings or any consent management tool used on the website.

6. Google Analytics (GA4)

With your consent, this website uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service provided by Google. Google Analytics helps us understand how visitors use our website by measuring metrics such as page views, session duration, and interactions. Google may process data such as IP-derived location information, browser and device data, and usage events. IP anonymization and privacy-friendly settings should be enabled where available. The legal basis is your consent under GDPR Article 6 (1) lit. a.

7. Meta Pixel

With your consent, this website may use Meta Pixel provided by Meta Platforms to measure the effectiveness of ads and improve our marketing activities. This may include collecting information about page visits, user behavior, and conversions. The legal basis is your consent under GDPR Article 6 (1) lit. a.

8. Recipients and international data transfers

We may share personal data with technical service providers and platform operators only to the extent necessary for the operation of the website, analytics, marketing, or communication. If data is transferred to countries outside the European Union or the European Economic Area, such transfers are carried out only on the basis of appropriate safeguards such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses where necessary.

9. Retention period

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the relevant purpose or as required by legal retention periods. Data that is no longer necessary is deleted unless continuous retention is required by legal obligations.

10. Your rights under the GDPR

You have the right to request information about your stored personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, and to object to processing where legal conditions are met. Where processing is based on consent, you can withdraw your consent at any time with future effect. To exercise your rights, please contact info@leolan.net.

11. Right to complain

If you believe that the processing of your personal data violates applicable data protection law, you have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

12. Updates to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect legal, technical, or operational changes. The version published on this website will apply.
