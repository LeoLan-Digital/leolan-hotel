Status and selection

This public overview lists possible recipients by function. Only providers required for the customer's actual configuration are used. A binding, versioned list is supplied before production use.

Infrastructure and transport

  • Cloudflare – security, DNS, CDN and transport.
  • Hetzner Online GmbH – hosting of workflow and integration components. LeoLan self-hosts the n8n software used there.

Communication and AI

  • OpenAI – external AI processing where enabled.
  • Resend – transactional email.
  • Meta Platforms / WhatsApp – messaging and, where enabled, marketing.

Payment and hotel systems

  • Stripe – payment processing.
  • Customer-selected PMS providers – hotel integrations after approval and compatibility review.

International transfers

Where a provider processes outside the EEA, safeguards required for the concrete processing are documented, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses.
